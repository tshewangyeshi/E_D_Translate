Passport renewal

Apply for a passport online and collect it from the nearest office.

Read the eligibility rules before you apply.

Applications are not accepted by email.

Fees and payment

Pay Nu. 500 by 30 June 2026.

ServiceFeeProcessing time
New passportNu. 1,500Within 10 working days
Passport renewalNu. 500Within 7 working days

What to bring

  • A copy of your citizenship certificate
  • Two recent passport photographs
  • Your old passport

Contact

For help, write to applications@portal.gov.example or visit the help page.

Office hours: Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm.

Legal notice: giving false information in a passport application is an offence under the law.
